Kruidnoten Cheesecake

Ingredients

  • 240 g kruidnoten (or speculaas cookies)
  • 100 g unsalted butter
  • 600 g cream cheese (Philadelphia)
  • 180 g granulated sugar
  • 4 medium eggs
  • 125 g sour cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 20 g flour
  • 4 tsp speculaas spice mix
  • 175 g kruidnoten (for folding into the batter)
  • Extra kruidnoten (for topping)
  • Toasted almond flakes (optional)

HOMEMADE SPECULAAS SPICE MIX

  • 8 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tsp ground cloves
  • 2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tsp ground coriander seed
  • 1 tsp ground anise seed
  • 1 tsp ginger powder
  • 3/4 tsp ground cardamom

Process

  1. Ensure all ingredients are at room temperature before starting — this improves cheesecake texture.
  2. Prepare the crust: finely grind kruidnoten and melt butter. Mix UNTIL a crumbly, sticky mixture forms.
  3. Press mixture firmly INTO a 24 cm springform lined with parchment paper.
  4. Make the cheesecake batter: beat cream cheese + sugar UNTIL smooth and creamy.
  5. Add eggs one at a time, mixing UNTIL incorporated.
  6. Add sour cream + vanilla extract. Mix UNTIL smooth.
  7. Fold flour + speculaas spices INTO the batter.
  8. Fold 175 g kruidnoten INTO the batter.
  9. Pour batter over the crust and smooth the top.
  10. Bake at 150°C for 60 minutes (top/bottom heat).
  11. Turn off the oven, leave the door slightly open, and let the cheesecake cool slowly.
  12. When only slightly warm, cover with foil and refrigerate overnight to set.
  13. Before serving, top with kruidnoten and optional toasted almond flakes. Add toppings only at serving time to prevent them from becoming soft.

Result

A creamy, spiced cheesecake with a crunchy kruidnoten crust and warm speculaas flavors — perfect for festive occasions.

Storage

  • Keep refrigerated for 3–4 days.
  • Toppings may soften over time; add them just before serving.
  • Freezer-safe (without toppings) for up to 3 months.

Tip

For best texture, bring all ingredients to room temperature before mixing. Add kruidnoten topping only at serving time to keep them crunchy. Note: I used chocolat kruidnoten for the topping in the photo. I bought them in the Netherlands. You can always try to make them yourself with help of the recipe 'Kruidnoten'
