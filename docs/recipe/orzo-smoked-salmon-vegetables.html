Orzo with Smoked Salmon and Vegetables

Ingredients

  • 500 g orzo
  • 400 g smoked salmon (or any preferred protein)
  • Vegetables of choice (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1–2 carrots, sliced
  • 1 zucchini, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • Cooking oil
  • 1 tub garlic herb cream cheese
  • Cooking cream (optional)
  • Italian herbs
  • Paprika powder
  • Black pepper

Process

  1. Finely chop the onion, garlic, carrots, zucchini, and bell pepper. Cut smoked salmon INTO bite-sized pieces.
  2. Heat a little oil in a pan. Add onion + garlic and cook UNTIL fragrant.
  3. Add carrots. Cook a few minutes UNTIL they begin to soften.
  4. Add bell pepper + zucchini. Cook UNTIL vegetables are tender.
  5. Meanwhile, cook orzo in a separate pot according to package instructions.
  6. Season vegetables with Italian herbs, paprika powder, and black pepper.
  7. Add garlic herb cream cheese. Stir UNTIL melted and creamy. OPTIONAL: Add cooking cream for a richer sauce.
  8. Let mixture simmer a few minutes UNTIL flavors combine.
  9. Add smoked salmon. Cook briefly UNTIL warmed through (do NOT overcook).
  10. Drain orzo. Add a splash of pasta cooking water to the vegetable pan if needed.
  11. Combine orzo with the vegetable-salmon mixture. Stir UNTIL evenly coated.
  12. OPTIONAL: Top with grated cheese before serving.

Result

A creamy, flavorful orzo dish with smoked salmon and colorful vegetables — easy, comforting, and perfect for busy evenings.

Tip

Use frozen vegetables to save time — no chopping needed. You can also swap salmon for chicken or minced meat; just cook those earlier in the process.
