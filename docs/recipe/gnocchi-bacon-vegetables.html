Gnocchi with Bacon and Vegetables

Ingredients

  • 500 g gnocchi
  • 150 g bacon pieces
  • Vegetables of choice (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1–2 carrots, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 100 g peas
  • Cooking cream (optional) or any sauce of choice

Process

  1. Heat a frying pan.
  2. Add the bacon pieces and cook UNTIL lightly crispy.
  3. Add the gnocchi and fry for a few minutes UNTIL golden.
  4. Prepare the vegetables: chop onion, garlic, carrots, and bell pepper. Add them to the pan.
  5. Add peas (or use frozen mixed vegetables to skip chopping).
  6. Season with paprika powder, Italian herbs, and black pepper.
  7. OPTIONAL: Add cooking cream or another sauce to make the dish richer.
  8. Let everything cook for a few minutes UNTIL vegetables are tender and flavors combine.
  9. Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve warm.

Result

A simple, comforting gnocchi dish with crispy bacon and colorful vegetables. Easy to customize and perfect for busy days.

Tip

Use frozen vegetables to save time. No chopping needed and just as tasty.
