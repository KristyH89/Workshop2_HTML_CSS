Strawberry Cheesecake

Ingredients

  • 300 g Bastogne cookies (or similar spiced cookies)
  • 100 g unsalted butter
  • 600 g cream cheese (Philadelphia)
  • 180 g granulated sugar
  • 4 medium eggs
  • 125 g sour cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 20 g flour
  • 2–3 tbsp strawberry jam (optional, for swirling into the batter)
  • Fresh strawberries (for topping)

Process

  1. Ensure all ingredients are at room temperature before starting — this improves cheesecake texture.
  2. Prepare the crust: finely grind Bastogne cookies and melt butter. Mix UNTIL a crumbly, sticky mixture forms.
  3. Press mixture firmly INTO a 24 cm springform lined with parchment paper.
  4. Make the cheesecake batter: beat cream cheese + sugar UNTIL smooth and creamy.
  5. Add eggs one at a time, mixing UNTIL incorporated.
  6. Add sour cream + vanilla extract. Mix UNTIL smooth.
  7. Fold flour INTO the batter.
  8. OPTIONAL: Swirl 2–3 tbsp strawberry jam INTO the batter for extra flavor.
  9. Pour batter over the crust and smooth the top.
  10. Bake at 150°C for 60 minutes (top/bottom heat).
  11. Turn off the oven, leave the door slightly open, and let the cheesecake cool slowly.
  12. When only slightly warm, cover with foil and refrigerate overnight to set.
  13. Before serving, top generously with fresh strawberries.

Result

A creamy, smooth cheesecake with a Bastogne cookie crust and fresh strawberry topping — perfect for summer gatherings or a festive dessert.

Storage

  • Keep refrigerated for 3–4 days.
  • Add fresh strawberries just before serving for best texture.
  • Freezer-safe (without topping) for up to 3 months.

Tip

For a stronger strawberry flavor, swirl strawberry jam through the batter before baking. Add fresh strawberries only at serving time to keep them firm.
