Kruidennoten (Dutch Spiced Cookie Bites)

Ingredients

  • 250 g flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 125 g brown sugar
  • 1½ tbsp speculaas spice mix
  • 125 g butter, room temperature
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 2–3 tbsp milk

HOMEMADE SPECULAAS SPICE MIX

  • 8 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tsp ground cloves
  • 2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tsp ground coriander seed
  • 1 tsp ground anise seed
  • 1 tsp ginger powder
  • 3/4 tsp ground cardamom

Process

  1. Place flour, baking powder, brown sugar, speculaas spices, butter, and salt INTO a bowl.
  2. Knead UNTIL a dough forms. Add milk as needed (usually 2 tbsp) UNTIL dough comes together.
  3. Do NOT over-knead. Shape dough INTO a ball, wrap in plastic, and refrigerate ≥ 30 minutes.
  4. Preheat oven to 180°C. Line baking tray with parchment paper.
  5. Roll small balls of dough (marble-sized) and place on tray. Press lightly to flatten.
  6. Bake 15–20 minutes UNTIL golden and crisp.
  7. Cool completely on a wire rack.

Result

Small, crunchy Dutch spiced cookie bites with warm speculaas flavors — perfect for Sinterklaas season or cozy snacking.

Tip

Use homemade speculaas spice mix for deeper flavor. For an easier version, use pre-mixed spices and make the dough ahead of time.
