Easy Healthy Ice Cream

Ingredients

  • 4 bananas, sliced and frozen (prepare a day ahead)
  • 150–200 g frozen strawberries
  • 25 ml heavy cream (or coconut milk)
  • Splash of vanilla extract

Process

  1. Slice 4 bananas and freeze them at least one day in advance.
  2. Place frozen banana slices, frozen strawberries, heavy cream (or coconut milk), and a splash of vanilla extract INTO a blender.
  3. Blend UNTIL smooth, creamy, and ice-cream-like.
  4. Serve immediately for soft-serve texture.

Result

A quick, creamy, and naturally sweet ice cream made from frozen fruit — healthy, refreshing, and ready in minutes.

Tip

Use frozen fruit to skip prep time. You can also swap strawberries for mango, blueberries, or mixed berries.
